# Simatu Network Onboarding — Experience Specification

**Status:** Documented for a future phase. Not implemented in the current phase.  
**Entry point:** The homepage floating **JOIN NETWORK** CTA.  
**Purpose:** Understand the applicant's current position, potential, interests, intent, and ways they could contribute to the Simatu network—not simply collect registration details.

## Experience principles

- Conversation-led, not a long form.
- Ask one controlled question at a time.
- Use the user's answer to determine the next question; keep branches relevant.
- Respect people starting with an idea, no business, limited resources, or no prior sales.
- Assess potential, integrity, willingness to learn, initiative, execution, and contribution—not wealth, registration status, employment, or business maturity.
- Keep answers structured internally while making the interaction feel natural.
- Explain what the network can offer and what participation may involve.

## Conversation flow

### 1. Personal introduction and intent
Understand who the person is, what brought them to Simatu, what they hope to achieve, and what they are looking for in a network.

### 2. Current business stage
Determine which path best describes the applicant:
- **Established business** — currently operating.
- **Early-stage business** — started building, testing, or selling.
- **Side hustle / occasional sales** — informal or inconsistent activity.
- **Idea stage** — an idea exists, but execution has not started.
- **No business yet** — interested in starting, but needs direction.

This selection is a routing signal, not a ranking.

### 3. Branch-specific discovery

#### Established business
Explore, progressively:
- Business name and description.
- Products or services.
- Website and social presence, if available.
- Current sales and consistency (optional context, not a gate).
- Current challenges and growth goals.
- What the applicant could contribute or collaborate on.

#### Early-stage / side hustle
Explore:
- What they are building or selling.
- Who they intend to serve.
- What they have tried so far.
- Obstacles and learning needs.
- Skills, resources, and support they can bring or need.

#### Idea stage
Explore:
- The idea and problem it solves.
- Intended customers.
- Any early validation or attempts.
- Skills, resources, obstacles, and next steps.
- What support would help them move toward execution.

#### No-business-yet pathway
Explore:
- Strengths, skills, interests, and areas of curiosity.
- Areas of interest such as fashion, beauty, technology, food, services, creative work, retail, or other fields.
- Business types or roles they would like to explore.
- Willingness to learn, experiment, and execute.
- Realistic time commitment and a near-term goal (for example, what they want to achieve in six months).

### 4. Network contribution and readiness
Use dialogue to understand:
- Skills and experience they can share.
- Initiative and follow-through.
- Collaboration and contribution mindset.
- Growth mindset and coachability.
- Integrity and reliability.
- Enterprise potential and interests.

Do not treat lack of capital, formal registration, an existing storefront, employment, or previous business ownership as automatic barriers.

### 5. Applicant profile and next step
At the end, consolidate the conversation into a structured applicant profile:
- Applicant summary and motivation.
- Current stage and branch.
- Business or idea details, when applicable.
- Skills, interests, and strengths.
- Goals, needs, and constraints.
- Potential contributions.
- Readiness signals and areas for development.
- Recommended pathway and rationale.

## Progressive pathways (working concepts)

These are guidance labels for future design—not final membership decisions or automated approvals.

- **Network Ready** — appears ready to participate and collaborate.
- **Development** — promising applicant who could benefit from guided development.
- **Incubation** — support to develop an idea or business from the ground up.
- **Contributor** — may add value through skills, creative, operational, technical, or other contributions.
- **Review Required** — requires a human review or clarification before a pathway is recommended.

Pathways should be flexible, revisited as the person progresses, and never presented as permanent labels.

## Potential Simatu support

Depending on the person's needs and pathway, the future network may explore:
- Brand or storefront integration and exposure.
- Business growth support.
- Skills development and collaboration.
- Incubation for people starting from zero.
- Contributor opportunities based on capability and interest.

These are future onboarding and network capabilities to design and validate; this document does not promise that each service is currently available.

## Scope boundary for this phase

Implemented now:
- Homepage floating **JOIN NETWORK** button as a visual CTA only.

Explicitly not implemented now:
- Onboarding chat or questionnaire.
- Branching conversation logic.
- Applicant data capture, persistence, or profile generation.
- Readiness scoring or automated pathway decisions.
- Authentication, submission, or backend/API changes.
- Navigation destination, route, or click action.

When onboarding is approved for implementation, it should be designed as a dedicated onboarding experience/engine rather than embedded in the homepage component.
