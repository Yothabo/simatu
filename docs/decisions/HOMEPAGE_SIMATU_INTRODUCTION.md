# Homepage Introduction: Simatu Positioning

**Status:** Approved direction  
**Scope:** Homepage section previously presented as Featured Brands  
**Implementation branch:** `codex/simatu-work`

## Decision

The section is an introduction to Simatu itself, not a portfolio or directory of its brands and ventures.

Its purpose is to begin explaining who Simatu is and establish the company's orientation towards building, opportunity and sustainable growth. It should introduce the company without attempting to explain every capability, venture or strategic detail at this stage.

## Approved language foundation

The introduction must remain consistent with the homepage hero positioning:

- **Building Beyond Convention**
- Bringing together technology, commerce and creative thinking.
- Shaping new ventures.
- Unlocking opportunities for sustainable growth.

The current introductory description is:

> Bringing together technology, commerce and creative thinking to shape new ventures, unlocking opportunities for sustainable growth.

Keep this language coherent across the homepage. Do not replace it with generic corporate claims or introduce unsupported statements.

## Experience requirements

- Remove the brand showcase and brand-specific slide content.
- Remove all venture destinations, site links and Visit Site calls to action.
- Do not use the section to promote or navigate to individual storefronts.
- Present Simatu as the subject of the section.
- Retain the established visual foundation where it supports the introduction.
- Allow future sections or subsequent content to explain the company's approach to growth in greater depth.

## Content boundary

This section is an opening introduction, not the complete company story. Do not add unapproved service lists, portfolio claims, business-unit descriptions, metrics or detailed growth frameworks. Further narrative development should follow explicit product direction.
